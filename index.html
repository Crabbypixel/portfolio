01 — WorkBuilt and shipped

MeraWealth

sole developer live since Sep 2026

Pre-IPO share trading platform · NestJS Next.js PostgreSQL Prisma AWS EC2

  • A real brokerage runs on this. I am the only engineer on it: 24 REST endpoints over a 7-table PostgreSQL schema covering KYC onboarding, the admin review queue the broker's staff work in, and a 6-state order lifecycle, from first commit through deployment and everything since.
  • Concurrent order matching is the part that has to be right. A per-company advisory lock serialises matching, SELECT FOR UPDATE row locks are re-validated inside the same transaction, and compare-and-set status updates return HTTP 409 rather than overwriting a decision someone else already made. A CHECK constraint holds pending = approved - matched at the database level, so share inventory cannot drift even if the application is wrong.
  • Login is passwordless email OTP, challenge-bound with an atomic single-use claim, on server-side sessions with per-route rate limiting and Helmet/HSTS. The matching rules are covered by 21 Jest unit tests.
  • The whole stack, Next.js SSR and NestJS and PostgreSQL, runs on a single 1 GB / 2-vCPU EC2 instance at 49% memory behind Nginx with PM2.

CrabbyGL

from scratch personal project

3D voxel game engine · C++20 OpenGL 3.3 GLSL CMake multithreading

  • A Minecraft-style engine with no engine underneath it: infinite chunk streaming, BFS flood-fill lighting, ambient occlusion, AABB physics, DDA raycasting, a day/night cycle and distance fog.
  • Binary greedy meshing merges runs of faces with bitmasks and cuts quad count 2.4× (58% fewer than exposed-face culling alone), taking vertex data from 11.8 MB to 4.7 MB. Gribb/Hartmann frustum culling drops 57.8% of loaded chunks every frame, 197 draw calls down to 83.
  • Generation, meshing and saving run on a 9-worker pipeline with double-buffered handoff and a per-chunk std::shared_mutex, keeping the render thread's critical sections O(1). Finding and fixing a save/reload race and a shutdown lost-wakeup hang was most of the work. 140 FPS 1% low on integrated Intel UHD graphics.
  • Water is a cellular automaton on a 20 TPS fixed tick: 7 flow levels plus falling water, order-independent two-phase updates that merge conflicting writes by strength, and a bounded BFS toward the nearest drop. A 15-case regression harness runs clean under ASan and UBSan.

Physics-Informed Neural Networks

research intern May – Jul 2026

CSIR Fourth Paradigm Institute, Bengaluru · Python PyTorch NumPy

  • Implemented PINNs in PyTorch that put the governing differential equation inside the training loss, using nested automatic differentiation to reach second-order residuals.
  • Built a variational PINN for the 1D transient heat equation from the weak formulation, with Gauss–Legendre quadrature and element-wise finite-element assembly, then tuned quadrature order, element count and gradient-biased time sampling against RMSE, relative L2 and maximum absolute error.
  • Applied the method to a fluid-conveying double carbon nanotube: a two-mode Galerkin projection to a coupled modal ODE system, stabilised by nondimensionalisation and Fourier feature encoding, checked against an independent matrix-exponential solution through modal trajectories and FFT eigenfrequencies.
